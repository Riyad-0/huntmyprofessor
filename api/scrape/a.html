Please note: The evaluation data is posted after all evaluation sheets are processed.
To search for an evaluation, please select the department, subject and course catalog number. Then click on the \"Search\" button to proceed.

To search by the semester or instructor's name, click on the link.

NOTE: This information has been provided to help you in planning and choosing your courses. It is only one source of information that should be used for this purpose.  We recommend that you also review course syllabi, the additional information made available via the Hunter online schedule of classes, and talk to other students. 

When interpreting these data please keep in mind the following:

  • Evaluations can vary widely for the same professor and may even differ across sections of the same course.
  • Not all students complete an evaluation.  Students who have withdrawn from a course or are not present on the day of the evaluation are not included in the calculations.
  • Ratings for course sections with a small number of respondents are not necessarily as reliable as those based on a larger number of students.
